Transparent Operating Model

How MargDrive Works With Cab Partners

MargDrive works with independent commercial cab operators and vehicle partners across India. When you submit a booking, our team coordinates with an appropriate partner based on route, vehicle category and availability. Once your booking is confirmed, we provide the relevant trip and driver details.

MargDrive does not directly employ the driver assigned to every booking.
