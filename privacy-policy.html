Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a search or booking request on MargDrive, we collect:

  • Full Name and Email Address
  • 10-digit Indian Mobile Contact Number
  • Travel details (Pickup and destination locations, dates, and times)
  • Pickup landmarks and special journey instructions

2. How We Use Your Data

The collected information is strictly utilized to:

  • Dispatch verified commercial chauffeurs to your designated pickup address
  • Transmit customer confirmation SMS updates and booking ID receipts
  • Facilitate customer service queries and route modifications

3. Data Security & Confidentiality

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers. All operational data transmission between client browsers and backend services uses industry-standard encryption protocols.

4. Contacting Us

If you have questions concerning your privacy or wish to request removal of your contact information, please email our Data Compliance Officer at privacy@margdrive.in.
