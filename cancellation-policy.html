Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Free Cancellation Window

You can cancel or modify your provisional or confirmed booking free of charge up to 4 hours prior to the scheduled departure time by contacting our 24/7 helpline at +91 90417 10472 or messaging our team on WhatsApp. The ₹500 advance confirmation amount will be refunded to your original payment mode within 3–5 working days.

2. Late Cancellation & Driver Dispatch

If a cancellation is requested less than 4 hours before the scheduled pickup time, or after the partner vehicle and driver have already been dispatched/arrived at the pickup address, the ₹500 advance fee is retained to cover driver mobilization and fuel expenses.

3. Modification Policy

Route changes, pickup time adjustments, or vehicle category upgrades can be requested prior to dispatch subject to partner vehicle availability. Fares will be recalculated transparently based on the new route distance.

4. MargDrive Partner Unavailability Refund

In the rare scenario where MargDrive cannot arrange an available commercial partner cab for your requested route/schedule, you will receive an immediate 100% refund of your ₹500 confirmation fee.

5. How to Request Cancellation

To cancel, please provide your Booking Request ID or official Booking ID (e.g. MD-20260926-0001) to our helpline at +91 90417 10472 or email support@margdrive.in.
