Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Free Cancellation Window

You can cancel or reschedule any confirmed booking free of charge up to 4 hours prior to the scheduled departure time by calling our 24/7 support helpline at +91 79737 85807.

2. Late Cancellation Fee

If a cancellation is requested less than 4 hours before pickup, or once the chauffeur has arrived at the pickup doorstep, a nominal vehicle mobilization fee of ₹500 may be charged to compensate the assigned driver for fuel and time.

3. Modification Policy

Route changes, pickup time adjustments, or car upgrades can be requested anytime prior to driver dispatch subject to vehicle availability. Fares will be recalculated according to the standard distance engine.

4. How to Request Cancellation

To cancel, please have your Booking ID (e.g. ROD-20260828-0001) handy and call +91 79737 85807 or message our support coordinator on WhatsApp.
