Please review the operational terms and policies governing MargDrive booking coordination services.

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Role of MargDrive and Partner Operating Model

MargDrive operates as an outstation cab booking and coordination platform. MargDrive connects customers seeking intercity, round-trip, local sightseeing, or airport transfer travel with independent commercial cab partners and operators across India. MargDrive does not directly employ the drivers assigned to bookings, nor does MargDrive directly operate every vehicle utilized for fulfilling travel requests.

2. Booking Request & Confirmation Process

Submitting a journey on the website creates a provisional booking request (e.g. PAY-REQ-XXXXXX). Our operations team coordinates with available commercial cab partners based on route and vehicle category. A booking is officially confirmed (and an official MargDrive Booking ID issued, e.g. MD-YYYYMMDD-XXXX) only after vehicle availability and the required advance confirmation payment are verified.

3. Fare Calculation, Advance Confirmation & Balance Payment

  • Base Fare Calculation: Displayed fares represent the estimated base fare covering the partner vehicle, fuel, and driver allowance for the selected route distance.
  • Advance Confirmation Amount: An advance payment of ₹500 is required to verify and confirm your booking request. This ₹500 confirmation fee is fully adjusted against the total trip fare.
  • Remaining Balance Payment: The remaining trip fare (Total Estimated Fare minus ₹500 advance) is payable directly to the assigned commercial cab partner/driver during the journey or upon trip completion.

4. Toll Taxes, State Border Permits & Parking Exclusions

Unless explicitly declared otherwise in a custom package, the base trip fare excludes highway toll taxes, expressway FASTag charges, state border entry permits, and airport/monument parking fees. All such statutory charges are payable directly by the customer based on authentic physical or electronic receipts generated during the journey.

5. Extra Kilometres, Route Deviations & Waiting Charges

If a customer requests additional deviations, extra stops, or travels beyond the scheduled itinerary, extra per-kilometer charges and waiting charges apply as per standard category rates (Sedan: ₹11/km; SUV/Innova: ₹15-18/km) and shall be payable directly to the partner driver.

6. Cancellation and Refund Policy

  • Customer Cancellation (>4 hours before pickup): Full refund of the ₹500 advance confirmation fee processed within 3-5 business days.
  • Customer Cancellation (<4 hours before pickup / Driver En-Route): The ₹500 advance is retained as a partner dispatch mobilization charge.
  • Unavailability / Partner Cancellation: If MargDrive is unable to arrange a suitable partner vehicle due to unforeseen constraints, the ₹500 confirmation amount is 100% refunded immediately without deductions.

7. Delays, Breakdown & Backup Vehicle Coordination

In the rare event of mechanical failure or breakdown during the trip, MargDrive will make reasonable commercial efforts to coordinate an alternative partner vehicle or arrange onward transport assistance. MargDrive is not liable for indirect or consequential losses resulting from traffic bottlenecks, adverse weather, landslides, or road closures.

8. Customer Code of Conduct & Safety

Passengers must observe respectful conduct towards the partner driver, comply with applicable motor vehicle regulations, not exceed the vehicle seating capacity, and refrain from transporting hazardous or contraband materials.

9. Contact and Grievance Redressal

For inquiries, modification requests, or feedback regarding your journey, contact our 24/7 Helpline at +91 90417 10472 or email support@margdrive.in.
