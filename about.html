Our Mission

Simplifying Road Travel Across India

MargDrive was founded with a clear, singular focus: to make outstation and intercity cab bookings transparent, predictable, and stress-free.

Who We Are

MargDrive is an outstation cab booking and coordination platform. We coordinate intercity, round-trip, airport transfer, and local sightseeing journeys across India by connecting travelers with independent commercial cab partners. Unlike traditional travel desks that operate on opaque pricing models or charge two-way fares for one-way journeys, MargDrive calculates clear distance-based fares with zero return fare penalties.

Our Operating Model

MargDrive works with independent commercial cab operators and vehicle partners across India. When a customer submits a journey request, our operations team coordinates with an appropriate partner based on route, vehicle category and availability. MargDrive does not directly employ the driver assigned to every booking or own every vehicle in the partner network.

Our Core Principles

  • Clear Fare Transparency: We provide straightforward distance-based base fares. Tolls, state permits, and parking are payable directly as per actual receipts.
  • Commercial Cab Partners: Journeys are fulfilled by independent commercial operators with commercial licensed vehicles and experienced partner drivers.
  • Dedicated Coordination Support: Real operations coordinators available via direct helpline (9041710472) and WhatsApp for seamless trip communication.

Our Commitment

Whether commuting for business, traveling for a family pilgrimage, or catching a scheduled airport transfer, MargDrive coordinates a dependable vehicle and reliable commercial partner driver for your journey.
